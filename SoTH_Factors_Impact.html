What the SoTH file actually contains

The superset spans three themes — Ecology (Air, Agro-ecology, Energy, Biodiversity/Forest, Soil, Water, Waste), Social (Gender & Inclusion, Health & Nutrition, Institutions, WASH, Migration, Education...), and Economy (Export-Import, Livelihood, Youth Employment...). It also has a cross-cutting Commons theme covering things like housing architecture, land fragmentation, night-sky visibility, and thermal microclimate that doesn't map cleanly onto any single pillar. This report focuses the comparison on what's ecologically relevant to the six-pillar model; Social and Economy items are noted only where they intersect the environment (e.g. WASH, migration, credit).

SoTH's version is a subjective scoring question; the existing Shannon-Weiner formula in the workbook is a computed index, so no change was needed there, but the overlap confirms the approach was the right one.

What was deliberately left out, and why

Roughly two-thirds of the SoTH superset (Social and Economy themes, plus most of Commons) sits outside a six-pillar ecological framework by design. These weren't missed — they were excluded on purpose.
